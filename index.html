// 01 — The Game

Deliberate. Lethal. Team-based.

Breachline is a round-based tactical FPS where coordinated squads fight over fortified objectives. Positioning, preparation and timing decide every engagement — not twitch alone.

Tactical Combat

Deliberate movement, meaningful positioning, a consistent damage model and clean sightlines. Every death is explainable.

Teamplay First

Roles, shared intel and combined utility beat solo mechanics. Designed around small coordinated squads.

Dedicated Servers

Server-authoritative networking from day one. No P2P advantages, competitive integrity enforced server-side.

Built on UE5

Unreal Engine 5 with modern rendering — Lumen, Nanite and virtual shadow maps target the desktop experience.
